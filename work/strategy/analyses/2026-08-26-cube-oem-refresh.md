## Question

What public facts about Cube / CubePlus OEM peers and the surrounding market changed (or stayed the same) since 2026-07-22?

## Scope

- In scope: Re-check the same OEM set (opTricon, Biosynex/Chembio, VHC, TargetVet, Hygiena, GADx/HeadStart) plus high-level IVDR/market-size citations.
- Out of scope: Invented TAM/SAM for homeDX; new smartphone-LFA competitor map; copying OEM clinical-performance numbers into homeDX claims.

## Method

- Sources used: company sites, 2026 Biosynex catalog, TargetVet store, Hygiena product pages, GADx/360Dx/ClinicalTrials.gov, plus third-party market/IVDR notes listed in [`../sources/2026-08-26-cube-oem-refresh.md`](../sources/2026-08-26-cube-oem-refresh.md) (accessed 2026-08-26).
- Assumptions: Form-factor + RFID still indicate Cube lineage where opTricon is not named. Paywalled market reports are **vendor estimates**, not primary filings.

## Findings

1. **opTricon story is unchanged and still the hardware baseline.** CubePlus pages, March 2025 datasheet, >40k readers / >300 OEM customers, Biosynex/Chembio partnership, and Android/iOS APP+API remain live. Europium fluorescence is still “in development.” The acquisition page still claims readers are “IVD and FDA approved” — record as manufacturer wording, not a verified catalog of FDA-cleared SKUs.

2. **Biosynex/Chembio kit+reader SKUs are still on sale.** 2026 catalog still lists **BIOSYNEX CubePlus** `CUBE-270-01` for RAPID-D. Chembio still sells **DPP Micro Reader** and **Micro Reader II** (CE-marked language on the II page). No public consumer cloud + Facharzt pathway found.

3. **VHC tightened its first-party assay set.** vitality-health-check.com now markets **one reader, two tests: Vitamin D and Ferritin** (not Vitamin D-only). Counseling / professional POC framing is unchanged. Distributor QuickREAD CUBE pages still claim extra markers (testosterone, cortisol, COVID antibodies) — keep those as **unverified distributor marketing**.

4. **TargetVet added a canine SKU; equine list price is unchanged.** Equine Cube Reader Plus remains **USD 800–875**. A **Canine Cube Reader Plus + K9 QP4 10-test** bundle is listed **AVAILABLE 8/2026** at **USD 1,000–1,075**. Still veterinary-only.

5. **Hygiena Cube is unchanged** (ASY3206, GlutenTox 3–40 ppm on Hygiena pages). Still food-safety QA, not human IVD.

6. **HeadStart is still not a live consumer Cube rival.** GADx describes it as a **prototype** (urine LFA + optical reader + app/cloud). NCT04296318 is **terminated** (business decision). 360Dx notes further IVDR/FDA studies before UK/US commercialization. Conceptually closer to homeDX’s *software* stack than in July — still pipeline.

7. **Market numbers: cite broad estimates, leave Cube SAM TBD.** Mordor: Europe IVD **USD 30.58B (2026)**. Paywalled: global rapid-test *readers* ~USD 1.2B (2025, Verified Market Reports); Europe *at-home testing* USD 8.7B (2026, MarkWide) — both much broader than Cube accessories. **SOM still unknown.**

8. **IVDR Class C gates are live this month.** For legacy Class C devices, (EU) 2024/1860 conditions include NB **application by 26 May 2026** (already past as of this note) and a **signed NB agreement by 26 September 2026**, with placing on the market into **31 Dec 2028** only if conditions hold. Confirm with PRRC against the regulation — this is industry context, not a homeDX status claim.

## Implications for homeDX

- Product / positioning: July conclusion stands — lead with **accessory + care pathway**, not “we make a Cube.” VHC’s two-test professional kit is the closest *human near-patient* analog; still no public teleconsult wrap.
- Go-to-market: Pharmacy/practice counseling (VHC) and kit+reader bundles (Biosynex) remain the GTM patterns to learn from. TargetVet’s canine launch is ecosystem news, not a patient rival.
- Competitive response: opTricon APP/API is still the “why not use the free app?” alternative. If HeadStart ever ships, it would be a **home reader + app + cloud** analog — watch, don’t treat as launched.
- Risks: IVDR Class C application window has closed for *legacy* devices seeking the 2028 runway; homeDX’s own classification is still a draft (product brief). Do not copy VHC/Hygiena/Chembio performance numbers.
