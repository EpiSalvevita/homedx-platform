## Comparison dimensions (default)

Use these when ranking vs. homeDX (Cube accessory + software platform):

- Device model (dedicated reader vs. phone camera vs. lab send-out)
- Software: account, history, payments/licenses, certificates
- Geography / regulatory posture (public claims only)
- Channels (DTC, pharmacy, clinic, B2B)
- Pricing model (device, per-test, subscription)
- Moat (hardware lock-in, brand, distribution, data)
